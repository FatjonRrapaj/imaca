About IMACA

Premium halal meat for the GCC

IMACA is a dynamic and forward-thinking company specialising in the import and export of premium halal meat products to the GCC (Gulf Cooperation Council) countries. We provide fresh, healthy and ethically sourced beef and lamb to meet growing demand in the region, and we stand at the intersection of superior product quality, regulatory compliance and outstanding customer service.

Behind IMACA is a team with decades of collective experience in international trade, supply chain management and the halal meat industry. From the moment the meat is sourced to its final delivery to our clients in the GCC, we protect both the integrity of the product and the satisfaction of our customers.

We prioritise trust, transparency and ethical practice, and we keep high standards of hygiene and food safety through production, distribution and delivery. Our goal is to be the preferred provider of halal meat in both quality and service.

Quality

Food safety and certification

Safety program

Our commitment to food safety, integrity and product quality supports our focus on the demands of international customers. Producers and their governments work together on strict standards and quality assurance through the whole supply chain, based on risk assessment and sound science, with independently audited systems.

Standards

Export-licensed processing plants operate under national standards for hygienic production and transport of meat for human consumption, based on world best practice and consistent with ISO 9001. HACCP-based quality assurance is mandatory for export abattoirs.

Laboratory

Microbiological analysis is carried out on raw materials and finished products using approved methods that follow food safety regulations. We monitor every stage, from sourcing to final distribution, so hygiene, safety and quality are maintained.

Certification

Our products are certified halal by an accredited authority recognised in the GCC region, under HACCP and ISO 9001 quality assurance. With every order we provide the certifications you need, including product quality, halal status and food safety.

Experience

Hands-on, from herd to shipment

Our team

We take a hands-on approach to every stage, working directly on the ground in the regions we source from. We are involved from the herding of livestock to careful slaughtering, processing, storing and transport, so quality holds through the whole supply chain and clients receive fresh product on time and in perfect condition.

Experience

We have exported meat to decentralised supply chain markets in Southeastern Europe, where logistics and distribution need adaptive strategies, and built efficient channels to a diverse range of customers with reliable, timely deliveries. In the GCC we supply top-tier meat to hypermarkets, cold storages and restaurants, and we keep strengthening our place in the region's food-service sector.

Our markets

We keep a strong understanding of global demand and build tailored programmes for each market and channel: trade, retail, food service, government and the end consumer. We also work with governments and other influential bodies to improve market access, overcome barriers and streamline logistics.
